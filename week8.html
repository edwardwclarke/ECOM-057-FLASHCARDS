What is a value investor?
Someone who buys companies for less than the value of their existing assets.
What does a contrarian investor do?
Buys unpopular “loser” stocks, believing the market has overreacted.
What are activist investors?
Investors who push for changes in how a company is run to increase its value.
What is a passive screener?
Someone who uses filters like low PE or PBV to find undervalued stocks.
What changes do activists push for?
Fixing bad assets, adjusting debt, improving leadership, and giving back cash.
What do contrarians need to succeed?
Confidence, patience, and ability to handle short-term losses or ridicule.
