Introduction
What is a value investor?
Like buying a shop worth £70 for just £50. You focus on current value, not future dreams.
Contrarian
What does a contrarian investor do?
Buys “loser” stocks. Like buying a forgotten toy, hoping it becomes cool again later.
Activist
What are activist investors?
They try to fix companies—like helping a messy lemonade stand run better.
Passive Screener
What is a passive screener?
Someone who uses rules (like checklists) to find cheap stocks with hidden value.
Activist
What changes do activists push for?
Selling bad assets, changing leaders, or returning unused cash to shareholders.
Contrarian
What do contrarians need to succeed?
Confidence, patience, and the ability to wait while others panic.
