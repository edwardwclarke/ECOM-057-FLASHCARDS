What is convergence trading?
A strategy where traders profit when the prices of two related assets move back together after diverging.
What is cointegration?
A long-term link between two assets where their prices stay close together, like a drunk man and his dog on a leash.
Why do active funds often underperform?
High fees, transaction costs, taxes, and too much trading reduce their returns compared to the index.
